The bank contains 75 original questions based on Chapter 8, Effective Communication. It covers purpose, audience, organization, FOCUS principles, writing principles, essay structure, staff studies, email professionalism, public speaking, résumés, interviews, and social media.

## Distractors
Do not simplify them. They intentionally test close distinctions such as thesis vs. topic sentence, facts vs. assumptions vs. criteria, manuscript vs. memorized vs. impromptu vs. extemporaneous, and résumé vs. interview purposes.

## Validation
Confirm 75 unique records, four choices per question, valid correct letters, aligned choice explanations, and preserved mnemonics, visual metadata, sources, and reinforcement IDs.
