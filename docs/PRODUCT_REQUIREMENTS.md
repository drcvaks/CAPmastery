# Product Requirements

## Purpose

CAP Mastery is an adaptive study coach for Civil Air Patrol cadets. The pilot serves Heshy and Avigail, with Chaim and Rachel as family adults, while preserving a path to families, squadrons, instructors, and more exams.

The product teaches source-supported concepts, identifies weak objectives, schedules useful review, provides explanations after answers, estimates readiness without promising exam outcomes, and uses supportive motivation.

## Version 1 outcomes

- Separate email/password accounts and database-backed roles.
- Leadership and Aerospace exam content organized by program, exam, course, volume, chapter, section, topic, and objective.
- Recommended, quick, focus-area, missed-concept, remediation, practice-test, and private challenge sessions.
- Multiple-choice and true/false questions with reviewed explanations and source citations.
- Secure server-side grading, attempt history, mastery, spaced review, readiness, streaks, achievements, goals, and family coaching.
- Responsive web administration for hierarchy, question editing/review, CSV import, reports, roles, links, and audit history.
- Android-first student experience with automated critical-logic and permission tests.

## Out of scope for Version 1

No AI API, automatic PDF ingestion or publication, public leaderboard, open messaging, CAP login integration, recalled protected exam content, payments, push notifications, app-store release, or broad squadron deployment.

## Users and success

- Students learn, see progress, and receive useful next actions without failure-focused language.
- Linked parents/coaches see only authorized students and coach rather than surveil.
- Reviewers publish only authorized, sourced, unambiguous content.
- Administrators manage access through audited workflows.

Pilot evidence includes session completion, objective accuracy, delayed retention, repeated-mistake reduction, practice-test consistency, voluntary return, usefulness, frustration, and question-quality reports. Adaptivity should not be evaluated seriously until each exam has broad objective coverage and approximately 150–250 reviewed questions.

## Product constraints

- Readiness is an unofficial study estimate and must say so.
- High readiness requires minimum content coverage.
- Practice tests withhold feedback until completion.
- Challenges remain private and recognize improvement, persistence, and completion as well as score.
- Every approved question has an authorized source, one objective, one best answer, plausible distractors, an explanation, and a citation.
